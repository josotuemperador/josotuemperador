Los Sugar Boys - Official Website

This is the official landing page for Los Sugar Boys, an emerging rock band from Argentina. The website provides fans with information about upcoming tour dates, the band's history, musical influences, and a multimedia gallery.
🎸 About the Band

Los Sugar Boys are a rock band focused on high-energy performances. Their sound is heavily influenced by:

    Grunge

    Stoner Metal

    Psychedelic Rock

🚀 Features

    Responsive Header: Includes a navigation menu and a mobile toggle button.

    Hero Section: High-impact call to action for upcoming events.

    Tour Schedule: A detailed table showing dates, times, and locations for concerts in Buenos Aires (CABA/GBA), Córdoba, and Rosario.

    Biography Section: A brief history of the band and their musical inspirations.

    Multimedia Gallery: A grid showcasing photos of the band performing and rehearsing.

    Social Media Integration: Links to Instagram, Spotify, and YouTube in the footer.

🛠️ Technologies Used

    HTML5: Semantic structure for better SEO and accessibility.

    CSS3: Custom styling (linked via styles.css).

    Google Fonts / Custom Favicon: Enhanced branding and visual identity.
